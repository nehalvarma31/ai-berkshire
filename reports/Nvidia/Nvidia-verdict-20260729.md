## Top Risks

1. **Competitive Erosion:** AMD gains design wins; Meta, Google, Tesla scale custom chips; ASP compression erodes economics faster than guidance implies.
2. **Valuation Mean Reversion:** Any miss on growth guidance triggers 20–30% drawdown; market pricing perfection with zero room for error.
3. **Data Center Saturation:** If enterprise AI adoption slows or capex cycles weaken, demand cliff appears faster than expected.
4. **Geopolitical & Regulatory:** China export restrictions tighten further; antitrust scrutiny on dominance in AI chips; fab supply fragility.
5. **Execution Risk:** New architectures (Blackwell, Rubin) fail to deliver expected perf/watt gains; yields underperform.
