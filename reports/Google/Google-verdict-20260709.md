## Top Risks

1. **Capex bet:** FY2026 capex guidance of $180–190B (vs. $91.4B FY2025) is a massive bet that AI/Cloud demand absorbs this infrastructure profitably — if not, FCF and ROIC both compress meaningfully.
2. **Antitrust:** Two adverse antitrust rulings (search monopoly remedies Sep 2025; ad-tech monopoly liability Apr 2025) are under appeal but could still force data-sharing or divestiture, eroding the core moat.
