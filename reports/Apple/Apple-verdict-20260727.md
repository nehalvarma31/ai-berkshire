## Top Risks

1. **iPhone concentration:** iPhone revenue concentration (~50% of sales) leaves Apple exposed to slower replacement cycles and China demand softness.
2. **Regulatory pressure:** App Store fees and antitrust action in the EU/US could erode high-margin services revenue over time.
