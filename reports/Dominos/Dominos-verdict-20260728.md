🏦 DOMINO'S PIZZA (DPZ) · NYSE

VERDICT: 🟡 WATCH
Reason: Exceptional business with fortress moat and stellar management, but valuation has compressed the margin of safety to uncomfortable levels.

── BUSINESS ──
What it does: Global pizza delivery franchisor operating 18,800+ stores across 90 countries, generating revenue primarily from franchise royalties and supply chain.
Moat: Brand recognition, network effects in delivery ecosystem, switching costs for franchisees, and proven operational playbook defensible against competition.
10-yr outlook: Continued international expansion and digital penetration should sustain mid-single-digit same-store sales growth with margin expansion.
