## Top Risks

1. **Amazon competition:** Continued grocery and logistics encroachment; AWS profitability dwarfs Walmart's entire earnings; risk of pricing wars crushing margins.
2. **Wage inflation:** Labor represents ~9% of revenue; sustained wage pressure from tight labor markets could compress already-thin retail margins despite pricing power.
3. **Macroeconomic sensitivity:** Prolonged recession or consumer pullback in discretionary spending could erode traffic and force promotional activity, damaging margins.
4. **Multiple compression:** Market may re-rate Walmart closer to 22–24x P/E if bond yields rise sharply or growth disappoints, resulting in 15–20% downside.
5. **International exposure:** Walmart's presence in Mexico, Canada, and Japan adds FX risk and geopolitical complexity; underperforming regions drag returns.
