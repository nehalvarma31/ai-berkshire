## Top Risks

1. **Subscription saturation in developed markets:** Mature markets (US, Canada, Europe) approach ceiling; growth increasingly dependent on emerging markets with lower pricing and higher churn.

2. **Password sharing enforcement and pricing backlash:** Crackdown on account sharing has added subscribers but faces subscriber resistance; further price increases risk churn acceleration.

3. **Content cost inflation and competitive escalation:** Streaming wars drive up talent/licensing costs; Disney, Amazon, Apple have deeper pockets; risk of margin compression if pricing discipline fails.

4. **Macro sensitivity:** Subscription discretionary spending vulnerable in recession; already seeing early churn signals in cost-of-living pressures.

5. **Ad tier monetization execution:** Ad business is growing but at lower margins; must balance ad load with subscriber experience to avoid churn.
