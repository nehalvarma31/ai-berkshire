## Top Risks

1. **Leadership vacuum plus national-security spillover.** The 33-year founder is gone and the interim CEO is a finance executive. The alleged foreign-intelligence link runs through a customer-linked contact, and lawmakers are already pushing for new limits on foreign chip sales while the investigation is still open.
2. **Peak-cycle earnings and concentrated customers.** Run-rate EPS (~$9.88) is about twice FY2026 ($4.90), so the P/E looks cheapest when risk is highest. The top 4 customers (61% of revenue) are building replacement chips, and neocloud customers NVIDIA helped finance are under stress (CoreWeave bonds yield 15%+).
